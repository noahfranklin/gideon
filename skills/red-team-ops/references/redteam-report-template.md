# Red Team Engagement Report — template

## Executive summary
- Objectives and whether achieved (flag status table)
- Business-risk narrative (plain language)
- Top strategic recommendations

## Engagement overview
- Scope, threat profile emulated, timeline, approach (announced/unannounced)

## Attack narrative (kill chain)
Step-by-step story from recon to objectives, with ATT&CK technique IDs.

## Findings
### [SEV] <Title>
- ATT&CK technique(s), affected assets, evidence (redacted)
- Reproduction, business impact, remediation
- Attack-path role (which paths it enables — see vuln-chaining)

## Detection-gap matrix (purple team)
| ATT&CK technique | Executed | Detected? | Alert quality | Recommendation |

## Prioritized remediation
Fixes ranked by number of attack paths broken.

## Appendix
Full activity timeline, touched systems, cleanup confirmation.
